FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do you supply only stationery?

No. InstiSupply is positioned as an institutional supplies business covering stationery, paper, hygiene, tissue, cleaning, pantry, printer consumables, PPE and other workplace essentials.

Can you quote against our existing RFQ or product list?

Yes. Send your descriptions, pack sizes, quantities and preferred brands. We can quote against your existing list and identify alternatives where appropriate.

Do you work with schools and hospitals?

Yes. The site is designed for education, healthcare, corporate, facilities-management and industrial buyers. Regulated medical products are supplied only where the relevant approvals and compliant sourcing requirements apply.

Can we arrange scheduled replenishment?

For recurring items, scheduled or call-off supply can be discussed based on volumes, commercial terms and delivery locations.
